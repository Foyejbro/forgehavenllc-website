1. Sujon's correct photo is bundled locally as assets/sujon.jpg, so there is no dependency on ../../images/worker2.jpg.

3. Scroll reveal uses IntersectionObserver and works without external libraries.

5. Services are interactive and include an in-page "Typical deliverables" detail list.
6. A separate "GRAPHIC DESIGN DETAILS" section clearly lists actual services and deliverables.

8. CSS includes prefers-reduced-motion accessibility fallback.
9. Project inquiry opens a structured WhatsApp brief.

NOTE ABOUT ONLINE STOCK IMAGES
I found suitable free graphic-design workspace references on Pexels, but this package intentionally does NOT hotlink third-party stock images. Hotlinks can break later or slow your site. You can later download licensed stock photos and place them inside assets/portfolio/ for stable hosting.
