CLIENT QUESTIONS

Answers before you order.

Useful for both Bangladesh-based and international clients.

Can I send only a reference or screenshot?+

Yes. A screenshot, old design, rough sketch or moodboard can help communicate direction. Explain what you like about it—layout, color, image treatment, typography or overall mood.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. Briefing, meetings, feedback and digital delivery can be handled remotely. Include your country, timezone and preferred communication method.

How is pricing decided?+

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, quantity, variants, dimensions, deadline, revision requirements and source-file needs. Scope is discussed before production begins.

What should I prepare before ordering?+

Your goal, target audience, exact text or copy, final usage, dimensions, brand assets, references, deadline and anything the design must avoid.

Can you improve an existing design?+

Yes. Existing artwork can be modernized, cleaned up, adapted to new sizes or developed into a stronger new direction when you have the right to use the supplied material.

Do you provide editable/source files?+

Source-file delivery should be agreed in the scope before production because requirements differ between branding, marketing, print and image-manipulation projects.
